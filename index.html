01 · Profile

About Me

An academic professional with over a decade of experience in teaching, research, academic coordination and student development.

I am Shikha Nagar, Assistant Professor of Economics at Manav Rachna University, Faridabad, and a UGC-NET qualified academician pursuing a Ph.D. in Economics. My professional interests span Economics, entrepreneurship, research, student career development and industry engagement.

Alongside teaching and research, I work to strengthen students’ professional profiles and improve their access to internship opportunities. I actively coordinate with industry organisations and seek to bridge the gap between academic learning and workplace expectations through meaningful industry connect and experiential exposure.

My approach to education emphasises analytical thinking, practical application, continuous learning and the development of career-ready graduates.
